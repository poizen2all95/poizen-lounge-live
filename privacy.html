Poizen Lounge

Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Poizen Lounge is a live sports-picks board run by PoiZen (Maciel's Inc.). This page explains, in plain words, what the Lounge collects and what you control. The short version: your pick outcomes are collected so the numbers stay honest; your dollar amounts are yours to share or keep on your own device; we never sell your data.

If you just watch the board

You can view the public board, the overlay and the Lab without an account. Nothing about you is saved to our database. Your browser keeps a few settings on your own device (your theme, team colors and alert choices).

To chat or play Pick'em, the Lounge gives your browser an anonymous ID. Chat messages and Pick'em names and picks are public to everyone on the board.

If you make a My Lounge account

Always collected, because this is what keeps the Lab honest:

  • Each slip you save: the players, stats, lines, More/Less, the DraftKings multiplier you entered, the payout multiple (what it pays compared to your entry), and the Lab's read at the time you saved it.
  • How it turned out: which picks hit or missed and whether the slip won or lost.
  • Your display name and the date you joined. You sign in with Google; Google and Firebase (Google's account service) handle your sign-in and your email address. We don't copy your email into the Lounge database.

Your choice, off by default:

  • Exact amounts (what you put in and what it pays). Off: they stay only on the device where you entered them. On: they sync to your account so they show on all your devices.
  • Letting PoiZen see your account (your slips, and your amounts if you synced them), so he can look at your play with you.

Responsible-play settings (your weekly limit and any cool-off) are saved to your account so they apply on every device.

Who can see what

  • You can see everything in your account.
  • PoiZen can see your display name, join date, number of slips, the choices you made above, and the outcomes listed under "always collected". He can see your full account only if you turned that on.
  • Nobody else. Other members and the public can't see your account. The database itself enforces this, not just the website.

We don't sell your data, rent it, or use it for ads. There are no ad trackers on the Lounge.

Services the Lounge uses

  • Google Firebase for sign-in and the database.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host it receives your IP address when you load a page.
  • ESPN: your browser asks ESPN directly for scores, box scores, injuries and game logs.
  • Google Fonts for the typefaces, and jsDelivr for the screenshot reader. A screenshot you read is processed in your own browser and is never uploaded.

Your controls

  • Export: My Lounge → Privacy → Export my data downloads everything in your account as a file.
  • Change your choices any time. Turning off amount sync removes your amounts from the account and keeps them on this device.
  • Delete everything: removes your account, your slips, your outcomes and your entry in the members list, and clears the amounts saved on that device. Chat messages you already posted stay public unless PoiZen removes them; ask and he will.

Age

The Lounge is for people 21 and older. We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone younger. If you think a minor has an account, let PoiZen know and it will be deleted.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new date, and My Lounge will ask you to review your choices if anything you agreed to changes. Questions or requests: message PoiZen in the Lounge chat, or @poizen2all95 on TikTok or YouTube.
