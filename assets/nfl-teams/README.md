# NFL theme assets

The `nfl-theme-assets.js` manifest records the official source URL for every local crest and photograph. Team identifiers follow the Lounge's canonical NFL abbreviations.

- 32 official ESPN team crests, 500 × 500 PNG.
- 30 verified 2026 home venues. The Jets/Giants and Chargers/Rams reuse their shared venue photograph.
- Stadium JPEGs fit within 1440 × 810 pixels and use quality 82 to limit transfer size. Load the selected team's photograph only.
- Home venue identity comes from a non-neutral 2026 home game's ESPN summary rather than franchise metadata, which still listed Buffalo's former stadium.
- Buffalo uses an official Bills photograph of the new Highmark Stadium from the June 2026 opening gallery. Las Vegas uses an exterior photograph from Allegiant Stadium's own website. Other photographs come from ESPN's venue records; their capture dates and visible sponsorship signage may be older than the venue metadata.

Prepared October 22, 2026. Source links remain in the manifest for later replacement or refresh.
